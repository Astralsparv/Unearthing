Welcome to The Unearthing, an Action & Exploration Fantasy game.

The aim of the game is to delve through the mines and mine resources to be able to sell them and upgrade your equipment.
Throughout the game, you may uncover new information about the story of the town that you find yourself in.


Controls

The game does not run in real time, and only updates when you make an action.

You input by writing up to 2 characters and pressing enter.

Leaving it blank will update the game (e.g: enemies will move around) and you will have no actions.

[Actions]

You can type 'w', 'a', 's' or 'd' to set the direction you want your action to be.

By typing a direction and pressing enter; you will move in that direction.

If you type 'q' aswell, e.g: 'wq', you will make your action UP (but you will not move physically).

Your action will be based on the held item, being a weapon or a pickaxe.

If you are holding a weapon, you will attack in that direction - damaging enemies around you.

If you are holding a pickaxe, you will mine in that direction - breaking the tile if possible.
